Game and save help

Okey Mobile is an Age 1 strategy campaign with 25 levels for people aged 13 and older. The information and features available depend on your installed build. Older offline-only builds keep campaign progress in local app data; online-enabled builds may use guest or platform accounts and may offer purchases.

  • If the app appears stuck or progress looks wrong, close and reopen it. Match-recovery data may restore a match that was in progress.
  • Guest progress may be lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Bind a guest account to the same-platform Google account on Android or Apple account on iOS before purchasing.
  • Android and iOS accounts and online progress are separate. They do not transfer between platforms.
  • Older offline-only saves are local and separate from an online account. Deleting an online account does not delete local saves, and clearing app data or uninstalling may erase local or guest data. Your device and platform control their backups and copies.

Account deletion

In an online-enabled build, open Settings → Account → Delete this platform account, then confirm Delete account. Active guest accounts can request deletion without binding to Google or Apple. If you cannot use the app, email us without reinstalling it or signing in. See the account-deletion instructions.

Contact support

Email Support@degenerationlabs.com. The publisher monitors this mailbox for support and account-deletion requests.

To help investigate a game issue, you may include the platform, app version, device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. For an account request, include only the platform and the minimum account reference needed to locate it, if available. An unbound guest without a reference may be difficult to identify from email alone.

Never send a password, one-time code, access token, purchase token, store receipt, or payment details. For purchase questions, use the relevant app store’s support route where it applies; do not email transaction credentials.

中文

游戏与存档帮助

Okey Mobile 是一款面向 13 岁及以上人群、包含 25 个关卡的 Age 1 策略战役。适用的信息和功能取决于已安装的版本。较早的纯离线版本将战役进度保存在应用本地数据中；启用联网功能的版本可能使用访客或平台账号，并可能提供购买。

  • 如果应用似乎卡住或进度异常，请关闭后重新打开。对局恢复数据可能恢复进行中的对局。
  • 卸载应用或清除应用数据可能导致访客进度丢失。购买前请将访客账号绑定到 Android 上的同平台 Google 账号或 iOS 上的 Apple 账号。
  • Android 与 iOS 账号及联网进度彼此独立，不能跨平台转移。
  • 较早的纯离线存档保存在本地，与在线账号彼此独立。删除在线账号不会删除本地存档；清除应用数据或卸载可能会删除本地数据或访客数据。设备和平台决定备份及副本的管理方式。

账号删除

在启用联网功能的版本中，打开设置 → 账号 → 删除当前平台账号，然后确认确认删除。当前有效的访客账号无需绑定 Google 或 Apple 即可申请删除。如果无法使用应用，也可以通过邮件申请，无需重新安装或登录。请查看账号删除说明。

联系支持

请发送邮件至 Support@degenerationlabs.com。发布方会监控此邮箱，以处理支持和账号删除请求。

如需协助调查游戏问题，你可以提供平台、应用版本、设备型号、操作系统版本以及问题简述。申请账号相关事项时，只提供平台和定位账号所必需的最少参考信息（如有）。没有参考信息的未绑定访客账号可能无法仅凭邮件定位。

切勿发送密码、一次性验证码、访问令牌、购买令牌、商店收据或支付详情。购买问题可在适用时通过对应应用商店的支持渠道处理；请勿通过邮件发送交易凭据。
