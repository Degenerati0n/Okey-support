Support

Help with Okey Mobile and the offline Age 1 campaign.

English · 中文

English

Offline basics

  • The Age 1 campaign, levels 1–25, works fully offline. It does not need an account or a network connection.
  • If the app appears stuck or progress looks wrong, close and reopen it. Match-recovery data may restore a match that was in progress.
  • If you want to keep your progress, save or export a backup before deleting the app, resetting, or clearing app data.
  • Deleting the app on iOS or clearing app data on Android removes local data. Resetting or clearing data may erase some or all progress, and a reinstall can still preserve data when an iOS offload or a platform restore brings it back.
  • Whether and how device or platform backups are made is controlled by your platform’s backup settings, not by the installed app version.

Before you contact us

Optional details that help us understand a report: app version, device model, operating system version, the steps that led to the problem, and a screenshot if it is useful.

Never send a password or other credentials. Support will not ask for them.

Contact

Email support: Support@degenerationlabs.com

See the privacy policy for how support messages and local data are handled.

中文

离线基础说明

  • Age 1 战役（第 1–25 关）可完全离线运行，不需要账号或网络连接。
  • 如果应用似乎卡住或进度异常，请关闭后重新打开。对局恢复数据可能恢复进行中的对局。
  • 如果你想保留进度，请在删除应用、重置或清除应用数据之前保存或导出备份。
  • 在 iOS 上删除应用或在 Android 上清除应用数据会移除本地数据。重置或清除数据可能会抹除部分或全部进度，而重新安装仍可能通过 iOS 卸载或平台恢复保留数据。
  • 是否进行设备或平台备份以及备份方式，由平台的备份设置控制，而不取决于安装的应用版本。

请勿发送密码或其他凭据。支持人员不会向你索取这些信息。
