Delete your Okey Mobile account

Instructions for online accounts and local-only saves.

English · 中文

English

Delete an online account in the app

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Choose Delete this platform account, then confirm Delete account.

This is available for an active signed-in account, including an active guest account. A guest does not need to bind Google or Apple before requesting deletion. A guest that has become inactive or has no usable session may be difficult to identify without an account reference.

When the request is accepted, access to that Okey Mobile platform account stops and its active server game state is cleared. Authentication or provider cleanup may continue asynchronously and may need retrying. Android and iOS accounts are separate: deleting one does not delete the other. This request does not delete your Google or Apple account, app-store records, local device saves, or separate copies managed by Google Play Games, Game Center, iCloud, or another platform service.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email Support@degenerationlabs.com. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in. Include the platform (Android or iOS) and only the minimum account reference needed to locate the account, if available. We may ask for proportionate information to verify and locate the account. An unbound guest without a reference may not be reliably identifiable from an email address or description alone; you do not need to bind it before asking for help.

Prepare an account-deletion email

The link opens a message compose window for you to review and send. It does not send a message or submit a request automatically. Never include a password, one-time code, access token, purchase token, store receipt, payment details, or full payment-card information.

What may remain after deletion

Limited transaction, delivery, refund, dispute, fraud-prevention, security, deletion, support, and restricted backup records may be retained only while needed for purchase validation or restoration, refunds or disputes, security, support, deletion records, or applicable legal obligations. Access is restricted to those purposes; records are deleted or anonymized when no longer needed. Provider backup copies age out through their backup cycles; no specific cycle duration is promised. These retained records do not keep the deleted account available for gameplay. See the retention section of the privacy policy.

Offline-only builds and platform access

Older offline-only builds do not have an online Okey Mobile account to delete. Use your device or operating-system app-data controls to remove local app data. This can erase local progress and settings; it does not request deletion of an online account or separately managed platform copies. If Apple sign-in access remains after an online account request, you may also manage apps using Sign in with Apple. Revoking platform access is supplemental and does not replace the Okey Mobile deletion request.

Publisher: Degeneration Inc
Account deletion and support: Support@degenerationlabs.com

中文

在应用内删除在线账号

  1. 打开设置 → 账号。
  2. 选择删除当前平台账号，然后确认确认删除。

当前已登录的账号均可使用此功能，包括当前有效的访客账号。访客申请删除前无需绑定 Google 或 Apple。已不活跃或没有可用会话的访客账号，在缺少账号参考信息时可能难以定位。

请求被接受后，该 Okey Mobile 平台账号将停止访问，其活跃的服务器游戏状态会被清除。身份验证或服务提供方的清理可能异步继续，也可能需要重试。Android 与 iOS 账号彼此独立：删除一个不会删除另一个。此请求不会删除你的 Google 或 Apple 账号、应用商店记录、本地设备存档，或 Google Play Games、Game Center、iCloud 及其他平台服务管理的独立副本。

通过邮件申请删除

如果无法使用应用，请发送邮件至 Support@degenerationlabs.com。无需重新安装应用或登录。请说明平台（Android 或 iOS），并只提供定位账号所必需的最少参考信息（如有）。我们可能会要求合理适度的信息来验证身份并定位账号。没有参考信息的未绑定访客账号，可能无法仅凭邮箱或邮件描述可靠定位；你无需先绑定账号即可寻求帮助。

准备账号删除申请邮件

点击链接只会打开邮件撰写窗口，供你检查并手动发送；不会自动发送邮件或提交申请。切勿填写密码、一次性验证码、访问令牌、购买令牌、商店收据、支付详情或完整银行卡信息。

删除后可能保留的信息

有限的交易、发放、退款、争议、防欺诈、安全、删除、支持和受限备份记录，仅在购买验证或恢复、退款或争议、安全、支持、记录删除以及履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。相关访问仅限于这些目的；不再需要时会删除或匿名化。服务提供方的备份副本会按其备份周期逐步淘汰；本政策不承诺具体周期。保留这些记录不会使已删除账号恢复游戏访问。请参阅隐私政策的保存期限部分。

纯离线版本与平台权限

较早的纯离线版本没有可供删除的 Okey Mobile 在线账号。请使用设备或操作系统的应用数据管理功能移除本地数据。这可能删除本地进度和设置，但不会申请删除在线账号或由平台单独管理的副本。如果在线账号删除后 Apple 登录权限仍然存在，你也可以管理通过 Apple 登录的应用。撤销平台权限只是补充步骤，不能替代向 Okey Mobile 提交删除请求。
