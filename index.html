Okey Mobile

Okey Mobile is an offline single-player tactical game. The release described here contains only the Age 1 campaign, levels 1–25, and works fully offline.

  • No account is required, and no network connection is needed for the Age 1 campaign.
  • Campaign progress, settings, and match-recovery data are stored locally on your device.
  • The described release does not bundle advertising, analytics, tracking, or payment SDKs, and it does not automatically upload gameplay to a developer-operated server.

Read the privacy policy or get support.
