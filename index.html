Okey Mobile

This site provides privacy, support, and account-deletion information for Okey Mobile, an Age 1 strategy game with 25 campaign levels for people aged 13 and older. The information that applies depends on the features present in your installed build.

  • Online-enabled builds may provide guest accounts, optional Google sign-in on Android, optional Apple sign-in on iOS, and purchases when available. Optional purchases may include diamond packs and a non-renewing 30-day monthly pass. Bind a guest account to the same-platform account before purchasing. Guest progress may be lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Older offline-only builds keep progress in local app data and do not have an online Okey Mobile account. Local offline saves are separate from online accounts.
  • The game described here contains no ads. Online and optional platform features may still involve data processing described in the privacy policy.

Read the privacy policy, get support, or follow the account-deletion instructions.
