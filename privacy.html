Privacy Policy

This policy explains how data is handled for Okey Mobile and for this website.

English · 中文

English

App data in the described release

This policy applies to Okey Mobile 0.1.0, the offline Age 1 release described on this site.

The release described here contains only the Age 1 campaign, levels 1–25, and is designed to run fully offline. It does not require an account or a network connection.

  • Campaign progress, stars, training, Knowledge, settings, and match-recovery data are stored locally on your device.
  • The described release does not bundle advertising, analytics, tracking, or payment SDKs.
  • The game does not automatically upload gameplay activity, match results, device identifiers, or save contents to a server operated by the publisher.
  • Revival after a defeat is free and does not use an advertising service.
  • The described release has no cash purchases, paid currency, or paid gameplay power.

Your choices and local data

Deleting the app on iOS or clearing the app data on Android removes the local data the app stores. The in-game Battle reset is not a full data-deletion control and does not remove all settings or app data.

  • Removing the app icon or using an iOS offload is not the same as deletion: offloading keeps the app’s documents and data so that it can be reinstalled later.
  • If you exported or copied a backup, that copy lives outside the app and must be deleted separately wherever you stored it.
  • Whether and how device or platform backups are made is controlled by your platform’s backup settings, not by the installed app version. This policy does not promise that operating-system backups are erased.

Voluntary support messages

If you contact support by email, the email address, message, and any attachments you send are used to respond to your request. Support messages are retained only as long as necessary for support and legal obligations. You can request deletion of a support conversation using the contact details below.

Please do not send passwords or other credentials.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process information such as IP address, request details, and browser information as part of serving the site, under GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages do not add analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, or third-party content.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date in the footer shows the latest revision, and material changes will appear on this page.

中文

所述版本的应用数据

本政策适用于 Okey Mobile 0.1.0，即本网站所述的可离线游玩的 Age 1 版本。

此处描述的版本仅包含 Age 1 战役（第 1–25 关），可完全离线运行，不需要账号或网络连接。

  • 战役进度、星级、训练、知识、设置和对局恢复数据保存在你的设备本地。
  • 所述版本不捆绑广告、分析、跟踪或支付 SDK。
  • 游戏不会自动将对局活动、对局结果、设备标识或存档内容上传到发布方运营的服务器。
  • 失败后的复活为免费功能，不调用广告服务。
  • 所述版本没有现金购买、付费货币或付费战斗力。

你的选择与本地数据

在 iOS 上删除应用或在 Android 上清除应用数据，会移除应用存储的本地数据。游戏内的 Battle 重置并不是完整的数据删除功能，不会移除所有设置或应用数据。

  • 移除应用图标或使用 iOS 卸载（offload）并不等同于删除：卸载会保留应用的文稿与数据，以便之后重新安装。
  • 如果你导出或复制了备份，该副本位于应用之外，需要在保存它的位置单独删除。
  • 是否进行设备或平台备份以及备份方式，由平台的备份设置控制，而不取决于安装的应用版本。本政策不承诺操作系统备份会被清除。

自愿发送的支持邮件

如果你通过电子邮件联系支持，我们会使用你发送的邮箱地址、邮件内容和任何附件来回复你的请求。支持邮件仅在支持与法律义务所需的期限内保留。你可以通过下方联系方式请求删除某次支持对话。

请不要发送密码或其他凭据。
