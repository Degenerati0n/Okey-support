Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-13 · This policy describes information handling for Okey Mobile and this website.

English · 中文

English

This policy applies to the features present in your installed build of Okey Mobile. The game is an Age 1 strategy campaign with 25 levels, intended for people aged 13 and older. The game described here contains no ads.

Information used by the app

  • Local-only builds. Older offline-only builds store campaign progress and settings in app data on your device and do not have an online Okey Mobile account. Your device and operating system control local backups and copies.
  • Online accounts. Online-enabled builds may create an anonymous identifier for a guest account and use it to save online game state. If you choose Google sign-in on Android, we receive a Google account identifier and may receive the email address, name, and avatar URL made available by Google. If you choose Apple sign-in on iOS, we receive an Apple account identifier and the email address Apple provides, which may be an Apple private relay address.
  • Game and purchase information. Online gameplay uses progress, game actions, account state, balances, items, energy, tasks, and pass entitlements. When purchases are available, store transaction and product records are processed to validate purchases, deliver or restore items, prevent duplicate delivery, and handle refunds or reversals.
  • Security and support. Authentication services may process session information such as IP address and browser or app User-Agent to operate and protect sign-in. If you contact support, we receive your message and any attachments you choose to send.

Guest play is available in online-enabled builds. Bind a guest account to the same-platform Google account on Android or Apple account on iOS before purchasing. Android and iOS accounts, purchases, and online progress are separate and do not transfer between platforms. Guest progress may be lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Why information is used

We use information to operate the features in your build: create and manage accounts, provide and sync gameplay, process or restore purchases, answer support requests, secure the service, prevent fraud or misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where applicable, contract is the basis for account, game, and purchase services; legitimate interests support service security and handling support; legal obligations apply to required records; and consent is used for optional features where the law requires it.

Providers and platform-managed information

Online-enabled builds may use Google and Apple sign-in, app-store payment services, and Supabase services for authentication and online gameplay. Optional Google Play Games features may involve Google processing gamer identity and SDK analytics or diagnostics. On Apple devices, Game Center or iCloud may store platform-managed game information if you use those features. These services process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Older offline-only builds keep local saves on the device instead of using an online Okey Mobile account.

Android builds that include Google Play Billing also include its diagnostic logging. Depending on SDK calls and device conditions, Google may receive technical billing diagnostics and app/device metadata, such as SDK and app versions and error codes. SDK initialization can occur before a purchase is completed.

Deleting an Okey Mobile account does not delete your Google or Apple account, app-store transaction records, or separate copies saved by your device, Google Play Games, Game Center, iCloud, or another platform service. See account deletion for what the request does and what may remain.

How long information is kept

We keep information only while it is needed for the purposes below, review retention periodically, and do not keep it indefinitely just in case.

  • Online account and game state: kept to provide the account and gameplay until an accepted account-deletion request. Once accepted, account access stops and active server game state is cleared.
  • Transaction, delivery, refund, dispute, fraud-prevention, and deletion records: limited to what is needed to validate or restore purchases, handle refunds or disputes, prevent duplicate delivery or misuse, document deletion, and meet applicable legal obligations. Access and use are restricted to those purposes. We delete or anonymize these records when they are no longer needed for them.
  • Authentication, session, IP, User-Agent, and security logs: kept only as needed to operate and protect the service, investigate a security incident, and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Support correspondence: kept only as needed to handle your request and follow-up, resolve a related dispute, and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Backups: access is restricted and backup copies age out through provider backup cycles. Deleted data is not restored to active use. The timing of provider backup cycles varies and is not specified here.
  • Inactive guest accounts: the same necessity criteria apply; we do not set an automatic dormant-account expiry period in this policy.

After account deletion, authentication or provider cleanup may continue asynchronously and may need retrying. Some transaction, refund, security, deletion, or backup records may remain for the purposes above; this does not keep the deleted account available for gameplay.

Security

We use HTTPS for network connections and access controls for service data. No online service or device can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can request in-app deletion from Settings → Account → Delete this platform account, then confirm Delete account. Active guest accounts can use this control without binding to Google or Apple. You can also request deletion by email without reinstalling the app or signing in. Read the deletion instructions.

Depending on local law, you may have the right to access or correct information, request deletion or a copy, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify and locate your account.

Children and international processing

Okey Mobile is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child under 13 has provided account information.

Service providers may process information outside your country. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we use the safeguards required by that law. Contact us with questions about processing relevant to your account.

This website and policy changes

This information site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP address, request details, and browser information to serve these pages under GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages themselves do not add analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, forms, or embedded third-party content.

We may update this policy as the app or our practices change. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

中文

本政策适用于你所安装 Okey Mobile 版本中包含的功能。游戏是一款面向 13 岁及以上人群、包含 25 个关卡的 Age 1 策略战役。此处所述游戏不含广告。

应用使用的信息

  • 仅离线的版本。较早的纯离线版本将战役进度和设置保存在设备上的应用数据中，没有 Okey Mobile 在线账号。设备和操作系统决定本地备份及副本的管理方式。
  • 在线账号。启用联网功能的版本可能会为访客账号创建匿名标识，用于保存联网游戏状态。在 Android 上选择 Google 登录时，我们会收到 Google 账号标识，也可能收到 Google 提供的邮箱、姓名和头像网址。在 iOS 上选择 Apple 登录时，我们会收到 Apple 账号标识及 Apple 提供的邮箱，该邮箱可能是 Apple 隐藏邮箱转发地址。
  • 游戏和购买信息。联网游戏会使用进度、游戏操作、账号状态、余额、物品、体力、任务及月卡权益。在提供购买功能时，我们会处理商店交易和商品记录，以验证购买、发放或恢复物品、防止重复发放以及处理退款或撤销。
  • 安全和支持。身份验证服务可能处理 IP 地址、浏览器或应用 User-Agent 等会话信息，以运行和保护登录服务。如你联系支持人员，我们会收到邮件内容及你选择附上的文件。

启用联网功能的版本可使用访客身份游玩。购买前，请将访客账号绑定到 Android 上的同平台 Google 账号或 iOS 上的 Apple 账号。Android 与 iOS 的账号、购买和联网进度彼此独立，不能跨平台转移。卸载应用或清除应用数据可能导致访客进度丢失。

使用信息的目的

我们使用信息来运行你所安装版本包含的功能：创建和管理账号、提供及同步游戏、处理或恢复购买、回复支持请求、保护服务、防止欺诈或滥用，并履行适用的法律义务。在适用情况下，提供账号、游戏和购买服务以履行合同为依据；服务安全和支持处理以合法利益为依据；法律要求的记录以履行法律义务为依据；法律要求时，可选功能以你的同意为依据。

服务提供方与平台管理的信息

启用联网功能的版本可能使用 Google 和 Apple 登录、应用商店支付服务，以及 Supabase 身份验证和联网游戏服务。可选 Google Play Games 功能可能涉及 Google 处理玩家身份及 SDK 分析或诊断信息。在 Apple 设备上，如果你使用相关功能，Game Center 或 iCloud 可能保存由平台管理的游戏信息。这些服务依据各自的条款和隐私实践处理信息。较早的纯离线版本将存档保存在设备上，而不使用 Okey Mobile 在线账号。

包含 Google Play Billing 的 Android 版本也包含该 SDK 的诊断日志功能。根据 SDK 调用和设备条件，Google 可能接收技术性计费诊断信息及应用/设备元数据，例如 SDK 和应用版本、错误码。SDK 可在完成购买前初始化。

删除 Okey Mobile 账号不会删除你的 Google 或 Apple 账号、应用商店交易记录，或设备、Google Play Games、Game Center、iCloud 或其他平台服务保存的独立副本。有关删除请求的处理及可能保留的信息，请参阅账号删除说明。

信息保存期限

我们仅在实现以下目的所需期间保存信息，定期审查保存情况，不会仅为以防万一而无限期保留。

  • 在线账号和游戏状态：为提供账号和游戏而保存，直至接受账号删除请求。请求被接受后，账号将停止访问，活跃的服务器游戏状态会被清除。
  • 交易、发放、退款、争议、防欺诈和删除记录：仅保留验证或恢复购买、处理退款或争议、防止重复发放或滥用、记录删除以及履行适用法律义务所需的信息。相关访问和使用受到限制；不再需要时，我们会删除或匿名化这些记录。
  • 身份验证、会话、IP、User-Agent 和安全日志：仅在运行和保护服务、调查安全事件及履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。
  • 支持邮件：仅在处理请求及后续事项、解决相关争议和履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。
  • 备份：访问受到限制，备份副本会按照服务提供方的备份周期逐步淘汰。已删除的数据不会重新投入日常使用。服务提供方的备份周期有所不同，本政策不指定具体时间。
  • 不活跃的访客账号：适用相同的必要性标准；本政策未设定不活跃账号的自动到期时间。

账号删除后，身份验证或服务提供方记录的清理可能异步继续，也可能需要重试。部分交易、退款、安全、删除或备份记录可能因上述目的继续保留；这不会使已删除账号恢复游戏访问。

安全

我们使用 HTTPS 进行网络连接，并对服务数据实施访问控制。任何在线服务或设备都无法保证绝对安全。

你的选择和权利

你可以在应用中打开设置 → 账号 → 删除当前平台账号，然后确认确认删除。当前可用的访客账号无需绑定 Google 或 Apple 即可使用此功能。你也可以通过邮件申请删除，无需重新安装应用或登录。请阅读账号删除说明。

根据适用的当地法律，你可能有权访问或更正信息、申请删除或获取副本、限制或反对处理、在处理依据为同意时撤回同意，以及向数据保护机构投诉。如需提出申请，请联系我们。我们可能会要求提供合理必要的信息，以验证身份并定位账号。

儿童与跨境处理

Okey Mobile 面向 13 岁及以上人群，不是为 13 岁以下儿童设计的。如果你认为 13 岁以下儿童提供了账号信息，请联系我们。

服务提供方可能在你所在国家以外处理信息。在适用法律要求跨境传输保障时，我们会采用该法律要求的保障措施。如对与你账号相关的信息处理有疑问，请联系我们。
